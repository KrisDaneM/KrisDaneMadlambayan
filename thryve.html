T H R Y V E

Thryve is an interactive wellness and lifestyle tracking application that allows users to monitor workouts, meals, hydration, and sleep in one organized platform. It encourages consistency and self-discipline through progress indicators, daily streaks, and achievement rewards. Users can also track their mood, set personalized goals, and gain insights into long-term health trends. With intuitive dashboards, real-time analytics, and helpful reminders, Thryve makes it easy to build healthy habits, maintain a balanced lifestyle, and stay motivated every day.

Thryve App Pages

Dashboard Page

This dashboard page displays and tracks your daily health and fitness data, including calories, sleep, water intake, and progress toward goals, helping users manage their overall health in one organized place.

Meal Page

This meal page allows users to track daily nutrition, manage meals, calories, and protein intake, with analytics to review and understand eating patterns over time.

Workout Page

Track physical activity by adding workouts, duration, and calories burned. Provides summaries and analytics to help users monitor their exercise progress over time.

Water Page

Monitor and manage daily water intake with visual progress, quick-add options, and logs to track hydration consistency over time.

Sleep Page

Log and monitor daily sleep habits, track progress toward goals, view weekly insights, and analyze sleep patterns to improve rest and consistency.

Achievements Page

Displays reward badges unlocked by completing health and fitness milestones, motivating users to maintain consistent progress and healthy habits.
