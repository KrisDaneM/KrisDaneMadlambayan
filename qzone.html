Q Z O N E

This is a group project website created as a practice site for QZone Carwash and Detailing. It allows users to browse available services, view pricing, make appointments, and learn about the company’s story and values. The site demonstrates how a business can present its offerings clearly and professionally online, with sections for services, promotions, and contact information. While it was developed as part of a learning exercise and not for actual commercial use, it provides a realistic simulation of a functioning carwash website and showcases the team’s skills in web design, layout, and user experience.

Qzone Pages

Home Page

This is the Landing Page of the Q-Zone Professional Detailers practice website. It introduces the business and its services, like car detailing, paint protection, and coatings. The page shows why choose Q-Zone, explains the service process from booking to completion, and displays recent projects. It also includes client testimonials, a FAQ section, and links to branches, giving visitors a clear and easy way to learn about the business.

About Page

This page introduces Q-Zone Professional Detailers and explains who they are. It shows the company overview, their mission and vision, and the story of how the business started and grew. It highlights why customers should choose Q-Zone, emphasizing premium quality, trusted professionals, affordable pricing, fast service, and years of experience. It also lists key achievements, branches, and contact information. This page helps visitors understand the business, its values, and its services in a clear and organized way.

Services Page

This page shows all the services offered by Q-Zone Professional Detailers. It lists different service packages including Basic, Premium, and Deluxe, along with pricing and what each package includes, such as exterior washes, interior cleaning, paint protection, and engine detailing. It also highlights special services like paint protection film, ceramic coating, headlight restoration, underbody coating, glass coating, and interior protection. The page helps visitors easily understand what services are available, their benefits, and the cost so they can choose the right option for their vehicle.

Employees Page

This page introduces the Q-Zone Professional Detailers team. It shows the founder, Jon Quizon, and explains how the business started from a small garage and grew into a trusted auto detailing service. The page also highlights the skills and specialties of the team, like paint protection, ceramic coating, and precision detailing. Each team member is listed with their role, contact info, and status. It also describes the company culture, emphasizing passion, teamwork, and professionalism, giving visitors a clear view of the people behind the services.
